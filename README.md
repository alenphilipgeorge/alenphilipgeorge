<p align="justify">As an AI Product Engineer at <b>M</b>, I work across agentic AI, backend architecture, distributed workflows and product infrastructure, building everything from context-aware AI copilots and multimodal ingestion and memory pipelines to real-time messaging and the backend for our cook mobile app.</p>

<p align="justify">My focus is the engineering behind useful AI: agents that work with real-world data, reliable orchestration, durable execution, state management, fault tolerance, and the infrastructure that keeps complex workflows running when things inevitably fail. Building an agent is only the beginning; making it dependable, recoverable and scalable in production is the real engineering challenge.</p>

<p align="justify">I enjoy working where AI, systems engineering and product thinking meet, owning the work from architectural decisions to deployment and improving what ships.</p>
